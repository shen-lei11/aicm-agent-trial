Client: Mid-size healthcare SaaS provider. The AI/IT team is building an internal agentic system to assist support analysts with ticket triage and draft responses.

Current state:
- Deployment: Active Development (pilot starts in 6 weeks).
- Users: Internal Employees only (support and compliance teams).
- AI system type: Retrieval-augmented LLM + agent tools (LangChain orchestration).
- Model: Claude 3.5 Sonnet via API; no fine-tuning or training on proprietary data.
- Tools/APIs: Jira (ticket updates), Salesforce (case summaries), internal KB search, Slack notifications.
- Data types processed: support tickets, customer names/emails, account IDs, chat transcripts; no PHI confirmed but may contain PII.
- Logging/monitoring: basic request logs in Datadog; no model output redaction or audit trail yet.
- Governance: no formal model risk assessment; no documented data retention policy for AI outputs.

Concerns stated by client:
- Data lifecycle and retention for agent outputs
- Tool invocation safety (preventing unauthorized Jira updates)
- Monitoring and detection of data leakage in model prompts/outputs
- Role-based access and approvals for automated actions

Unknowns (explicitly not confirmed):

- Whether outputs are used for automated customer communications
